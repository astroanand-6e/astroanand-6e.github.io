Nov 1, 2026Local LLMs

Benchmarks vs. the crowd

Where the numbers and r/LocalLLaMA agree: LFM3-1.7B and the 3D-Coder are the real deals of the month. Where they split: DeepSeek V4.1 Flash wins every chart, but the crowd says it feels about on par with Qwen 3.8 Flash Next and needs a server-class RAM budget. The d1 decision models confuse people who expect a chatbot. And the Omni and Audio models impress on speed while users keep reminding everyone that a 2B brain is a 2B brain.

The takeaways

  • Best daily driver under 2 GB: LFM3-1.7B. The instruction-following and code scores are absurd for its size, but give it retrieval for facts.
  • One model for eyes and ears: LFM3-Omni-2B. Pick Audio-2B instead if you only need voice.
  • Agent plumbing: d1-2B as a millisecond router or classifier in front of a chat model.
  • Long-context RAG and chat: Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-E4B.
  • Frontier at home: DeepSeek V4.1 Flash at 2-bit holds its benchmark scores, if you have the disk for it.
