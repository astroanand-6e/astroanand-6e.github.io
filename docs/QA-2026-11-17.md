## Upgrade plan (UI/UX, interactivity)

Principles (ui-ux-pro-max: portfolio grid pattern): visuals first, the work within one or two screens of the top,
one primary action per view, motion that means something, everything keyboard-reachable, reduced motion honoured.
Brand stays: pitch black, Ferrari red, Instrument Serif, Geist, Geist Mono.

1. **Hero, shorter and fixed.** Cut the pinned film to about 6–7 viewports (intro, the eye beat, the burn into the
   work) and move the long version behind "Watch the full film". Fix the name overlap and the phone crop. New
   consistent avatar plates (ChatGPT vs Gemini comparison, Anand picks).
2. **Work section with media.** Each card gets a poster that plays a short muted loop in view (screen recording or
   a raea render), a one-line "how I built it", and real links (repo, demo, paper). Filter chips: All, Research,
   Products, Web. A card opens a case-study drawer: problem, approach, result, stack, links.
3. **Paper implementations page** (`/papers/`) plus a teaser row on the home page. Each entry: the paper, what was
   re-implemented, what is faithful and what changed, results against the paper's own numbers, one interactive
   figure where it explains the idea (e.g. an MAE mask-ratio slider, a β-VAE latent traversal), code link. An
   honest "next up" queue for papers in progress. Only verified repos and numbers.
4. **"How I build" section.** The AI-assisted workflow shown, not claimed: a deterministic terminal replay of a real
   change to this site (prompt, plan, diff, test, commit), with numbers read from git (commits, files, checks run).
5. **Command palette (⌘K / Ctrl K).** Jump to any project, paper or section; copy email; open résumé. Shows web
   craft and helps skimmers.
6. **Polish.** Brighter station figures, engineering stats in place of filler, chapter rail hidden over the index,
   the two Lighthouse fixes, a check at 375 / 768 / 1024 / 1440 in both themes and with reduced motion.
